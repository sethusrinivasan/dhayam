# ✺ Welcome to the Dhayam Wiki

**Dhayam (தாயக்கட்டை)** is the ancient Tamil race-and-capture board strategy game, recreated for modern browsers with real-time multiplayer, 3D cuboidal brass dice physics, and solo computer play.
