# 📜 Dhayam Rules & Strategy Guide

## Overview & Objective

Dhayam is played by 2 to 4 players on a 7×7 grid of 44 positions. Each player controls 6 pawns starting in reserve. The objective is to race all 6 pawns around the outer perimeter, transition to the inner track (unlocked by capturing an opponent), and land exactly in the center lotus goal (**D4** / பழம்).

---

## 1. The Dayakattai Dice (தாயக்கட்டை)

Two long rectangular cuboidal brass dice are cast. Each die has 4 faces: `0`, `1`, `2`, and `3`.

| Outcome (Die 1 + Die 2) | Value | Traditional Call | Special Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0 + 1` or `1 + 0` | **1** | **தாயம் (Dhayam)** | **Bonus throw** + Unlocks pawns from reserve. |
| `0 + 2`, `1 + 1`, `2 + 0` | **2** | இரண்டு | Normal roll. |
| `0 + 3`, `1 + 2`, `2 + 1`, `3 + 0` | **3** | மூன்று | Normal roll. |
| `1 + 3`, `2 + 2`, `3 + 1` | **4** | நான்கு | Normal roll. |
| `2 + 3` or `3 + 2` | **5** | ஐந்து | **Bonus throw**. |
| `3 + 3` | **6** | ஆறு | **Bonus throw**. |
| `0 + 0` (Double Zero) | **12** | **பன்னிரண்டு** | **Bonus throw**. |

### Bonus Chains
Rolling a **1, 5, 6, or 12** grants an immediate extra roll. All dice values accumulated in the turn are pooled and can be used together or divided across different pawns.

---

## 2. Key Rules of Movement

1. **Entering from Reserve**: Pawns can only leave reserve and enter their starting square (`A4`, `D7`, `G4`, `D1`) on a roll of **1 (தாயம்)**.
2. **Safe Squares (மலை / ✿)**: The 8 floral squares are protected sanctuaries. Opponents on safe squares co-exist peacefully and cannot be captured.
3. **Captures (வெட்டு)**: Landing on an unprotected opponent pawn on an unsafe square captures it, sending it back to reserve. **At least one capture is required before any of your pawns can turn into the inner ring.**
4. **Center Finish (பழம் / ✺)**: Pawns must land on **D4** by exact count.

---

## 3. Winning Strategy Tips

* **Team Progression**: Advance pawns in pairs or packs rather than leaving a single pawn isolated ahead.
* **Overcrowding Relief**: If 2 or more pawns stack on the same square, advance one out to avoid being blocked by enemy double-stacks.
* **Sanctuary Leaping**: Use safe squares (**மலை**) as stepping stones before leaping through vulnerable enemy territory.
